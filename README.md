Churn Analysis & Customer Intelligence

An end-to-end customer churn analytics project for an OTT subscription platform, designed to identify high-risk customers, understand churn drivers, and quantify the resulting revenue and CLTV impact.

Project Overview

The project integrates customer, subscription, and support data from relational tables using PostgreSQL and Python. The analysis covers data cleaning, feature engineering, customer segmentation, churn analysis, behavioral patterns, and executive-level business insights.

Key Highlights
Built 20+ business KPIs covering churn, retention, ARPU, tenure, revenue at risk, CLTV, and support intelligence.
Identified an overall 28.6% churn rate and 71.4% retention rate.
Found that monthly-contract customers churned at 55.6% vs. 8.3% for annual contracts — a 6.7× difference.
Quantified approximately ₹73.94/month in MRR leakage and ₹2,047 in CLTV erosion among six high-risk customers.
Identified September 2024 and Karnataka as key churn concentration areas.
Analyzed the relationship between complaints, escalations, CSAT, and customer churn.
Developed risk-based customer segmentation to prioritize High, Medium, and Low-risk customers.
Translated analytical findings into a targeted retention and contract-migration strategy.
Tech Stack

PostgreSQL | SQL | Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn

Business Impact

The analysis moves beyond simply measuring churn by answering three critical business questions:

Who is likely to churn? → Why are they churning? → Who should the business save first?

The final recommendations focus on high-value at-risk customers, monthly-to-annual contract migration, support recovery, and targeted retention campaigns, demonstrating how data analytics can directly support customer retention and revenue protection.
